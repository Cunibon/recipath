Honey Garlic Chicken Stir-Fry
Serves 4

Ingredients:
- 600 g chicken breast, cut into strips
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 thumb of fresh ginger, grated
- 3 spring onions, sliced
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 300 g rice
- Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear.
2. Cook the rice in a covered pot over low heat for 18 minutes, then leave it to rest off the heat for 10 minutes.
3. Whisk the soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar and cornstarch together in a small bowl to make the sauce.
4. Cut the chicken breast into strips and season it with salt and pepper.
5. Heat the sesame oil in a wok over high heat.
6. Sear the chicken strips until golden on all sides.
7. Add the minced garlic and grated ginger and stir for one minute until fragrant.
8. Pour in the sauce and let it simmer for 4 minutes until it thickens and coats the chicken.
9. Scatter the sliced spring onions over the top and serve with the rice.
